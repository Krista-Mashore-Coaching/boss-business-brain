# Start here

This folder builds your AI Business Brain: real files that teach any AI who you are, how you talk, who you serve, and what makes you different. You own every file, forever, on your own machine.

## Open the guide for your computer

Two guides are sitting in this folder. Open the one that matches your computer and follow it. Do not read both.

- **On a Mac:** open **`INSTALL-MAC.pdf`**
- **On a Windows PC:** open **`INSTALL-WINDOWS.pdf`**

They are the same steps written twice, once for each machine, because half of these steps genuinely differ and guessing wastes your morning. There is a `.md` version of each too if you prefer plain text.

## The short version, if you would rather just go

**1. Make sure this folder is really unzipped.** On Windows that means right-click the zip, Extract All, then Extract. Looking inside a zip is not the same as extracting it, and Claude cannot read a folder that is still zipped. On a Mac, double-clicking the zip is enough.

**2. Open the Claude app on your computer.** Not claude.ai in a browser. If you do not have it, get it at [claude.ai/download](https://claude.ai/download).

Then click **Code** at the top, start a **new** session, and click **Select folder**. Choose this folder, the one with `START-HERE.md` sitting directly inside it. If you see another folder with the same name inside, go one level in and pick that one.

**3. Click Trust when Claude asks.** It only asks once, and it is Claude protecting you, not something going wrong.

**On a Mac you will see a second box, from your Mac rather than from Claude**, saying Claude would like to access files in that folder. Click Allow. Same idea, and it also only asks once. **On Windows there is no second box.** If you are waiting for one, stop waiting.

That's it. You don't need to type anything special. Say hi, or say nothing at all, and it starts on its own.

## Two things that make people think it broke

**1. It talks a lot while it works, and a lot of it sounds like errors.** *"I found a problem, fixed it."* *"That was missing, installing it."* That is the system working, not breaking. You are installing more than a hundred employees at once, and like training a hundred real people on day one, things go sideways. The difference is BOSS finds and fixes its own problems while you watch, and says so. Let it run. It stops and asks you clearly when it actually needs you.

**2. When something really is wrong, do not email anybody.** Screenshot it, drag the screenshot into BOSS, and say "fix this." It repairs its own errors more often than not. Still stuck, bring it to office hours. That is what they are for.

**One naming thing:** the app is called Claude. Your system is called BOSS, and it lives inside Claude.

Real files, in this same folder, that you wrote nothing into by hand. Point any AI at this folder later and it will know your business.
