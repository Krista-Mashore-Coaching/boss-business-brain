# Start here

This folder builds your AI Business Brain: real files that teach any AI who you are, how you talk, who you serve, and what makes you different. You own every file, forever, on your own machine.

## Three steps

**1. Unzip the file.** Wherever it landed is fine. Downloads, Desktop, anywhere you can find it again.

On Windows, right-click it and choose **Extract All**, then Extract. Looking inside a zip without extracting it is not the same thing, and Claude cannot read a folder that is still zipped.

**2. Open the Claude app on your computer.** Not claude.ai in a browser. If you don't have it, get it at [claude.ai/download](https://claude.ai/download).

Then click **Code** at the top, start a **new** session, and click **Select folder**. Choose the folder you just unzipped, the one with `START-HERE.md` sitting in it.

If you open that folder and see another folder with the same name inside it, go one level in and pick that one instead. You want the folder that has `START-HERE.md` directly inside, not a folder that just contains another folder.

**3. Click Trust when Claude asks.** It only asks once, and it's Claude protecting you, not something going wrong.

**On a Mac you may see a second box, from your Mac rather than from Claude**, saying Claude would like to access files in that folder. Click Allow. Same idea, and it also only asks once.

That's it. You don't need to type anything special. Say hi, or say nothing at all, and it starts on its own.

## What you'll walk away with

Real files, in this same folder, that you wrote nothing into by hand. Point any AI at this folder later and it will know your business.
