# Setting up BOSS on a Mac

This is the Mac guide. If you are on a Windows PC, open `INSTALL-WINDOWS.md` instead. Following the wrong one will send you looking for things your computer does not have.

Two parts. Part 1 is your Business Brain. Part 2 installs your Operating System, and only applies once you have bought it.

**You need:** your laptop, and Claude Pro or higher. The free plan will not work.

**One naming thing before you start.** The app you download is called Claude. Your system is called **BOSS**, and it lives inside Claude. When this guide says BOSS, it means your system talking back to you.

---

# What it looks like while it's working

Read this part before you start, because it is the thing that makes people think they broke something.

**BOSS talks while it works.** It will say things like *"I found an error,"* *"that path was wrong, fixed it,"* *"this one is missing, installing it now."* Sometimes for a while, and sometimes fast enough that you cannot read it all.

**That is the system working. It is not the system breaking.**

You are installing more than a hundred employees at once. Train a hundred real people on their first day and things go sideways all day long. The difference here is that BOSS finds its own problems, fixes them while you watch, and tells you it did. The talking is the repair.

Let it run. It stops and asks you clearly when it needs something from you, and that is the only time you have to do anything.

---

# When something actually is wrong

Three things, in this order.

1. **Take a screenshot** of whatever you are looking at.
2. **Give the screenshot to BOSS and ask it to fix itself.** Drag the image straight into the same session and say *"fix this."* It reads its own errors and repairs them more often than not. This is the one to try first, every time.
3. **Bring it to office hours** if it is still stuck. That is exactly what office hours are for, and somebody else has usually hit the same thing.

**Do not email anyone about it.** Not Krista, not the team. It does not get you an answer faster, and the answer is almost always number 2.

If you are in the room right now, raise your hand.

# Part 1: Build your Business Brain

### 1. Download the file on your LAPTOP

Open a browser on the laptop you are going to work on and go to the address on the screen.

Do not scan the QR code with your phone. The file has to land on the laptop. If you already scanned it on your phone, no problem: open your email on the laptop and the download link is in there.

Enter your name, your email, and the password on the screen.

### 2. Unzip it

The file is `BOSS-AI-Business-Brain.zip` and it lands in your Downloads folder.

**Double-click it.** A folder appears right next to it with the same name. That folder is the one you want.

Wherever it landed is fine. Downloads, Desktop, anywhere you can find it again. Do not pull any files out of it.

### 3. Open the Claude app

The app on your computer, not claude.ai in a browser. If you do not have it, get it at **claude.ai/download**, then drag Claude into your Applications folder.

### 4. Point it at your folder

Click **Code** at the top. Start a **new** session. Click **Select folder**.

Choose the folder you just unzipped, the one with `START-HERE.md` sitting directly inside it.

If you open that folder and see another folder with the same name inside, go one level in and pick that one.

### 5. Click Trust

Claude asks once whether you trust this folder. Click **Trust**. That is Claude protecting you, not something going wrong.

### 6. Your Mac will ask a second time. Click Allow.

This is the step that throws people, so read it before it happens.

macOS controls which apps can see your Desktop, Documents and Downloads. The first time Claude reads a folder in one of those, **your Mac** puts up its own box: *"Claude would like to access files in your Downloads folder."*

Click **Allow**. It is a different box from the Trust one, it arrives second, and it looks like an error. It is not. It only asks once.

If you clicked Don't Allow by accident: Apple menu, System Settings, Privacy and Security, Files and Folders, then switch Claude back on.

### 7. Say hi

That is it. You do not type anything special. Say hi, or say nothing at all, and it starts on its own.

It asks you questions and writes your files while you answer. About 90 minutes.

**Want to talk instead of type?** Hold the microphone key on your keyboard and answer out loud. Most people go a lot faster this way.

### 8. Create your free GitHub account when it asks

Near the end it walks you through it and saves your username. Two minutes.

## When you finish

**Keep that folder. Do not move it, do not rename it, do not delete it.**

It is your brain, it is your backup, and you open it again in Part 2.

# Part 2: Install your Operating System

Only once you have bought it. Do these in order. Nothing works if you skip one.

**First: sign your contract and make your down payment.** Nothing unlocks until both are done, because the automation checks for the signed contract.

1. **Scan the code at the back table** to open the access form. Your phone is fine for this one.
   - Give your GitHub username. Not your email, not a link.
   - Choose **Agent/Lender** or **Other**. This picks which system you get.
2. **Submit.** The invite fires automatically. You do not email anyone.
3. **You do not have to accept the invite right now.** It waits in your GitHub account and you accept it during setup. It shows up at github.com under your notifications once you are signed in.
4. **Open the SAME folder** you built your brain in. Not a new download.
5. **Type:** `start phase two`

From there your AI does the whole install and tells you when to do something.

At the end it tells you to open a new session in your new folder and type `set up my OS`. Do that and you are finished.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| "You need to upgrade" | You are on the free Claude plan. Claude Pro or higher at claude.ai |
| Nothing happened when you opened the folder | You opened the zip itself, or the wrong folder. Pick the one with `START-HERE.md` directly inside |
| A box appeared saying Claude wants to access my files | That is your Mac, not an error. Click Allow |
| I clicked Don't Allow | Apple menu, System Settings, Privacy and Security, Files and Folders, switch Claude on |
| It is asking me the questions again | It did not find your Day 1 folder. Open the original one |
| No GitHub email | You do not need it. Sign in at github.com and check your notifications |
| I scanned the QR on my phone | Open your email on your laptop. The link is in there |
| "404" or "not found" | You accepted the invitation while signed into a different GitHub account |
| A "Create PR" button appeared | Ignore it. Close it with the X |
| I need to quit and reopen Claude | `Cmd + Q`, or the Claude menu, Quit |


Go back to **When something actually is wrong** at the top: screenshot it, hand it to BOSS, ask it to fix itself. Office hours if it is still stuck. Never email.
